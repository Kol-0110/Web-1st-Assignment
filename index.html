What We Offer: Core Platform Capabilities

Our complete software ecosystem covers every crucial operational role in the hospitality lifecycle:

  • Front-Counter Cashier POS: Rapid order entry, split checks, tax calculations, and fast credit card or cash tender.
  • Tableside Waiter Handhelds: Mobile table ordering, seat-specific meal modifications, and immediate digital dispatch to the kitchen.
  • Kitchen Display Systems (KDS): Paperless line-cook display screens that organize pending orders by prep station and cooking time.
  • Human Resources (HR): Shift scheduling, digital time clocks, tip pooling distributions, and staff attendance logs.
  • Sales & Financial Analytics: Live revenue tracking, menu category breakdowns, discount auditing, and automated daily closeout reports.
  • Floor & Shift Management: Real-time table occupancy maps, server station allocations, and supervisor override approvals.
  • Owner Business Intelligence: High-level profit margin analysis, multi-branch comparisons, and remote mobile oversight.
  • System Administration: Granular role-based security permissions, hardware terminal pairing, and menu catalog management.

Proven Operational Workflow

Implementing BistroFlow in your restaurant streamlines daily service into four synchronized steps:

  1. Order Placement: Servers enter guest orders directly at the table using mobile handheld terminals or stationary front-counter registers.
  2. Instant Kitchen Routing: Tickets appear immediately on the Kitchen Display System (KDS), routed directly to the grill, fryer, or pantry stations.
  3. Order Fulfillment: Line chefs bump orders as each course is plated, instantly alerting waitstaff that dishes are ready for pickup.
  4. Seamless Settlement: Cashiers close out tickets with itemized receipts, automatic tax calculations, and multiple payment options.
