Who We Are & What We Offer

At BistroFlow, we develop specialized hospitality technology designed to unite front-of-house hospitality, back-of-house culinary production, and administrative leadership into one harmonious workflow.

Traditional restaurant setups rely on disconnected cash registers, handwritten ticket slips, and paper shift schedules. Our software replaces those bottlenecks with instantaneous digital communication. Every table order, ticket bump, shift punch, and payment transaction updates in real time across the entire venue.

Role-Based Management & Administrative Capabilities

Alongside our interactive frontline tools, BistroFlow incorporates comprehensive back-office and leadership modules designed to give managers, executives, and administrators complete operational oversight right from this platform:

Human Resources (HR) Module

How It Works: The HR module automates employee scheduling, digital clock-in and clock-out via employee PINs, and overtime tracking. It aggregates server gratuities for fair tip pooling distributions, manages staff leave requests, and generates automated payroll compliance summaries for weekly wages.

Sales Performance & Revenue Module

How It Works: The Sales module records every completed transaction in real time. It calculates net revenue, tracks beverage versus food category margins, highlights peak service hours, monitors promotional discounts or employee voids, and generates automated end-of-day reconciliation reports for accounting.

Floor & Shift Manager Module

How It Works: The Manager module provides floor supervisors with a real-time visual table map showing table turnover and active seating zones. Shift managers can authorize price discounts, reassign dining sections, monitor kitchen queue bottlenecks, and complete mandatory shift-opening and closing checklists.

Restaurant Owner Portal

How It Works: The Owner module gives restaurant proprietors high-level executive intelligence accessible from any mobile browser or laptop. Owners can review high-margin dishes, track overall venue profitability, compare multi-location revenue trends, inspect ingredient supplier costs, and monitor business health without being physically present on the floor.

System Administration & Security Module

How It Works: The Admin module controls the fundamental system architecture. Administrators configure role-based access control (RBAC) permissions, pair new wireless POS and tablet devices, set municipal sales tax rates, create and update menu catalog prices, configure receipt templates, and inspect audit logs for internal fraud prevention.

Our Core Engineering Principles

Every feature inside BistroFlow is developed following four foundational pillars:

  1. Zero Training Curve: Intuitive touch controls designed so new seasonal waitstaff and cashiers can begin taking orders in under 15 minutes.
  2. Local Network Offline Mode: If internet connectivity drops, local registers and kitchen screens continue operating without interruptions.
  3. Instant Data Synchronization: Order modifications made at a table sync immediately with the kitchen line within milliseconds.
  4. Strict Data Security: Industry-standard data encryption and individual PIN authorizations safeguard sales records and guest information.
